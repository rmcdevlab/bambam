## Decision and implementation

The owner's amendment supersedes the earlier articulated approach (D-179). Restore complete pose images, but do not restore the old two-layer compositor or its 24 ms overlaps. Each frame synchronously clears the entire canvas, including transparent pixels, and draws one 362 px sprite cell. No pose opacity animation, image blending, previous-frame layer or separately moving body part is used. Decode all six sheets before starting.

Sequence: greeting 0–1.86 s (two raised-paw waves without restarting the arm lift), unfold 1.86–2.46 s, briefly read 2.46–3.00 s, look up and lift 3.00–3.90 s, reveal the problem card and extend an open palm 3.90–5.10 s. Pending overview data holds downward-looking frame 41. Final frame is 71. Existing skip/fallback rules and card actions remain. Frame timing and order are in `admin-mascot-motion.ts`.

No new artwork is generated for the amendment. Use the existing registered v6 sheets, retaining their original identity, transparency and ground line. The rejected rig component, generated rig asset and unused prompt file have been removed from the working changes.

## Amendment 2 (owner, 2026-11-24): smoother pose changes, plain login button

- **S29 motion.** The leopard sheets (D-181) jumped at every sheet boundary, because each sheet was generated separately. The fix still shows one frame on one cleared canvas, with no blending:
  1. Each boundary cuts where the two poses are closest.
  2. The first frames after a cut take the scale and offset that match the previous frame.
  3. Over the next few frames, the image eases back to its registered position.

  These transforms are baked into `bam-leopard-admin-{3..6}.webp`; play order and timing are in `admin-mascot-motion.ts`.

The hint under the button already says to use the company account. The button still starts RMC SSO; behaviour is unchanged. Other texts that name RMC SSO (help dialog, maintenance notice, logout dialog, profile) are not changed by this amendment.
